How a search is put together

heavy omni mechs with at least 2 lrm 20 and no ppcs

weight class · omni or not · unit · with · comparator · number · equipment · and / or · without

Every part can be left out as long as there's a unit or some equipment: lams and heavy mechs are whole searches, and without a unit (with an xl engine) every unit is searched. Capitals and extra spaces don't matter.

In front of the name. Without one, Inner Sphere and Clan versions count together.

In front of a launcher's name, after any tech: launchers with it. artemis is IV or V.

And, or

Part What you can type Example
Joining equipment and, or, but (the same as and). With nothing in between, it's and. mechs with 2 ppc and a large laser
mechs with 2 ppc or 3 large lasers
Without a list without carries on through the list: none of any of it. It stops at something with its own number, or at with. Say without again for "either one missing". mechs without ppcs or lasers
mechs without ppcs or lasers but with 2 lrm 20
mechs without ppcs or without lasers

Equipment

Only what official units carry: the equipment mounted on the mechs and vehicles in the Master Unit List, as MegaMek's unit files list them. An item is missing if no official unit has it, and its tech only shows IS or Clan if some official unit carries that version.

Every piece of equipment some unit carries, under the groups a search can name. Each group's number is how many items are in it. Slang is shorthand; other names are MegaMek's names for it. Any of them works in a search.
